Your design workspace

Explore: pan and zoom the board, focus a screen and click prototype buttons to follow flows. All actions are simulated.

Edit: click text, edit its wording, size, color, weight or alignment in the inspector, then Apply edit. Change screen density or drag its header to reposition it.

Comment: click an element to attach feedback, or use the screen header for a screen-level note. Comments can be resolved and reopened.

Save: edits and comments save in this browser on this address. They are not written back to source files or synchronized with other people. Export review lets you copy or download a JSON backup. Import review restores it after you confirm.

Handoff: export notes for a readable screen-by-screen change log. Send the JSON to your implementer to replay the exact edits. Mockup approval is not implementation or security approval.

Keyboard: canvas arrows pan; +/− zoom. Controls remain keyboard accessible. Use the screen index if the board is too small.
