## 2. What the services are

- **The extension** estimates the electricity, carbon and water behind your chats on supported AI sites. It counts in your browser and keeps its records there.
- **The hosted service** lets an organization collect the numbers of its members' connected browsers and see them on an admin site.
- **Self-hosting:** an organization can run the server software itself under its open-source license. We do not operate self-hosted servers. The organization that runs one is responsible for it.

The figures are estimates, not measurements. They follow the published methodology based on EcoLogits and come with a range. They are not a verified emissions report. If you use them in reports or decisions, you are responsible for checking that they are suitable for that purpose.
