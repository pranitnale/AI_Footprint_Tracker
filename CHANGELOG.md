# Changelog

This file records user-visible software, contract and methodology changes. The current candidate has not been released.

## [Unreleased] - 0.1.0 candidate

### Added

- An agreement before anything is tracked (owner decisions of 17 and 18 October 2026, for the Chrome Web Store user data policy). A welcome page opens on install, and once after an update for installations from before this step: "See the footprint of your AI chats", with "Everything runs on your computer." as its most prominent message, how it works in three points, the "Load full chat history automatically" choice (on by default) and Agree and start or Not now. Until the person agrees, nothing new is captured, counted, recorded, read from a site or uploaded (estimates of records already saved in the browser may still be recalculated locally), the popup asks for the same agreement, and the bar shows only "Tracking is off" with Set up, the one exception to the compact bar the owner approved. Settings says when the person agreed and offers Turn off tracking to withdraw, separate from Pause. The dashboard overview, Settings and Details say that everything was calculated on this computer, or in organization mode that only numbers are shared. The Connect dialog asks for its own agreement, kept with its text version and time, and leads with what is never shared. The website home page leads with "Runs entirely on your computer." and where the data goes. The privacy policy describes the agreement.
- A short tour on the welcome page after Agree and start (owner request of 21 October 2026), also shown when the page opens after the person agreed elsewhere. Six steps, each with a screenshot or drawing and numbered highlights: go back to the AI tool and chat as usual (links to the five supported sites, reload a tab opened before installing); the bar under the message box and its figures and short states; Details; pinning the tracker from the browser's puzzle-piece Extensions button and the popup; the dashboard pages and what Settings offers; and what stays private. The dashboard Overview shows the "calculated on your computer" card at the bottom, with Read the methodology.
- The developer panel stays signed in for 7 days without activity, like organization administrators (owner decision of 14 October 2026). Because it reaches every organization, suspending or restoring an organization, assigning a plan, granting, revoking or replacing an exception, cancelling or reviewing a plan change, approving a domain and deleting a contact request now need a fresh email code sent to the developer's configured address, asked for in each confirmation. A retry with the same idempotency key replays the first answer without a new code. Only the administrator who created an export can read or download it; its id in the audit log no longer opens it for others. The data processing agreement lists the new actions.
- Organization administrators stay signed in on the website for 7 days from sign-in, without a shorter idle limit (owner decision of 14 October 2026). Signing out, suspension and removal of administrator access still end the session at once, and sessions made before the update keep their 24 hours. Promoting a member or removing administrator access now needs a fresh email code, like suspension, browser removal, erasure and organization deletion (review item S7). The Privacy policy and the data processing agreement say so.
- When a site shows a chat without loading its saved history, the tracker reads that chat itself, once per visit, with the same request the page uses and the person's login in that page: a claude.ai chat from the page's cache, a Cowork session whose start was not shown, a Gemini chat reopened in the same tab, a Perplexity thread after a full page load, a notebook reopened in the same tab, and a long ChatGPT chat whose earlier turns load only when scrolling (read page by page, at most 20 pages; pacing as changed on 17 October 2026, see Changed). It also reads once when the history reader refused the page's own read, unless its request would be the same. It runs only while the tab is visible, after a short wait, tries once more after a network error, time-out, 429 or 5xx (a minute or more later), and never retries refusals. The text stays on the device. While it runs, Details shows "Sending request" or "Receiving data"; a failure is named in Details. Owner decisions of 13 and 14 October 2026.
- The resource bar shows only the figures and the Details control (owner decision of 14 October 2026). Errors, update and progress states moved into Details: actions with their buttons at the top, progress in a line at the bottom, everything else under "Not everything could be counted". Until a chat has figures the bar shows one short state instead of the labels (owner decision of 14 October 2026, afternoon): "Waiting for your request" when there is nothing to count yet (new chat, start page, empty notebook), "Processing" while a reply streams or is counted, or an opened chat's history is read or calculated, and "No figures yet" when the chat has messages that could not be counted, with the reason in Details. Figures appear with the first counted part of a chat, and while anything updates the bar keeps the last figures.

- Model labels on every site map to the model registry. An unknown variant, such as GPT-6 Luna mini, Gemini 3 Pro Image or a Claude Sonnet mini, is estimated with the same variant's newest model in the library (GPT-5.4 mini, Gemini 3 Pro Image Preview), or with the newest model of the same kind from the same provider when the library has no version of that variant (Claude Haiku 5.5 for a Claude mini, nano or lite model; owner decision of 14 October 2026). A reasoning or speed word in a label, such as Thinking, Instant, Fast or High, is a setting of the model, not another model (owner decision of 14 October 2026): Claude Haiku 6 Thinking is estimated as an unlisted Claude Haiku (Claude Haiku 5.5), a Perplexity id ending in `_fast` as its model, and Gemini 3.8 Flash Thinking as 3.8 Flash; Details names the label, for example, "Claude Haiku 6 (Thinking)". Details names each model and the record behind it in plain words: o3-pro reads "o3 (Pro reasoning)", estimated as GPT-6 Astra, and Perplexity Best says that Perplexity chose the model and did not say which one.

- Claude token counts are multiplied by 1.56 (owner decision of 15 October 2026). Claude divides text into more tokens than the o200k tokenizer the tracker counts with. Measured on 15 October 2026 with Anthropic's `messages.count_tokens` on the repository's 400 synthetic texts (`calibration/tokenizer-golden.json`), less the 8 tokens Anthropic adds to every message, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Haiku 5.5 and Fable 5.1 gave identical counts at 1.63 times the o200k count for English, 1.97 for German, 1.43 for code, 1.35 for numbers and 1.56 over all texts; the owner chose one factor. It applies to every Claude count the tracker makes itself: claude.ai and Claude Cowork prompts, replies, shown reasoning, tool input, research, file text and the context advice, and Perplexity replies by a Claude model (the Computer orchestrators included). Counts a provider reports (Cowork `modelUsage`, AI artifact runs), uploaded image tokens, media and other models are unchanged. Each counted part is multiplied and rounded half up on its own, and a total adds the rounded parts, so totals stay the sum of their parts. The factor is `claude_token_factor` in `config/estimation.media.json`, part of the EcoLogits method definition, so this is a new method identity and earlier results keep their method. Stored counts stay as the tokenizer measured them: token figures apply the factor when they are added up, in the extension and on the organization server alike, so messages recorded earlier show it at once, and their electricity, carbon and water change when they are recalculated under the new method. Effect (own calculation with the EcoLogits core, modelled time, midpoint): a Claude Opus 5.5 reply counted as 1,000 tokens is now 1,560 tokens, 4.606 to 7.140 Wh, 1.770 to 2.745 gCO2e and 17.3 to 26.8 mL, about 55 % more; Claude Sonnet 5.5 1.491 to 2.303 Wh. Details says how Claude counts are made; the bar is unchanged.
- GLM models reached through Perplexity are estimated for US data centres (owner decision of 15 October 2026). Perplexity serves GLM itself (its Agent API lists `perplexity/glm-5.3`), says it hosts open models on US servers, and GLM 5.2 is reported to run on Nvidia B200 GPUs in the US; the operator is not public. Besides the United States electricity mix, they now use EcoLogits 0.11.2's own US multi-cloud data-centre factors, PUE 1.09 to 1.14 and WUE 0.13 to 0.99 L/kWh (its `huggingface_hub` provider configuration), instead of the PUE 1.16 and WUE 0.09 of Mistral's Swedish data centres, whose provider the catalog files the GLM records under. GLM 5.2 uses the `zai-glm-5-2` record instead of `glm-5-2`: same 744B total and 40B active parameters, but no serving speed measured on Mistral's platform, so both GLM versions use modelled serving speed. Effect (own calculation with the EcoLogits core, midpoint scenario): a GLM 5.3 reply has about 3.9 % less electricity and carbon and about 8.8 % more water (1,000 output tokens: 3.959 to 3.805 Wh, 1.522 to 1.463 gCO2e, 12.7 to 13.8 mL). A GLM 5.2 reply without a measured generation time now uses EcoLogits' modelled serving speed instead of the faster speed measured on Mistral's platform, so it shows about 37 % more electricity and carbon and 55 % more water (1,000 tokens: 2.786 to 3.805 Wh); with a measured time of 20 s for 1,000 tokens, about 2.2 % less electricity and carbon and 10.7 % more water. This is a new EcoLogits method identity; earlier results keep their method.

- Organization server speed (owner-approved speed work, 15 October 2026). The API runs four worker processes instead of one, so a slow summary no longer holds up sign-ins, uploads, configuration or health checks; each process keeps at most 12 database connections (8 plus 4 overflow) and checks a pooled connection before use. `GET /v1/config` no longer waits for the organization's lock (held by uploads and the maintenance worker's tick) unless it has to save a changed policy. Request log lines go to one file per UTC day (`requests.log.YYYY-MM-DD`) that every worker process appends to, and files older than 14 days are deleted as before. The web proxy compresses the website's files and summary, breakdown and request-list answers (never sign-in, session or token answers), caches content-hashed `/assets` files for a year, revalidates pages on every visit and downloads after five minutes, waits 95 s for summaries so the 90 s client limit applies, and reuses its connections to the API.
- Extension popup and dashboard speed with a long history (15 October 2026). With months of use the popup took about 9.5 s to show the person's own 30-day figures. Every reply a period counts made the background check the whole calculation state twice, read its saved input twice and hash the same records once per total, day, tool, model and chat. A scan now checks the calculation once at its end, reads a page of up to 200 replies with their parents, saved inputs and results together, hashes each record once for all its totals, reads only the requests and replies of the period it counts and, in the recalculated view, skips the original-method archive it never uses. On a synthetic profile in the test container the popup's first load fell from 70 s to about 10 s and a 30-day breakdown from 25 s to about 2.5 s (fake-indexeddb, so browser times are lower; see `apps/extension/test/perf/`). Figures are unchanged: `summary-parity.test.ts` compares every popup and dashboard answer on three profiles with the answers of the code before.

### Contracts and migrations

- New server settings `person_analytics_enabled` and `ip_collection_enabled` (both `false` by default; reported read-only as `features` in `/v1/config`, outside the policy). With person analytics off: `GET /v1/admin/requests`, `GET /v1/admin/users/{user_id}/requests/{request_id}`, `GET /v1/admin/network` and `POST /v1/admin/network/access` answer 404; `GET /v1/admin/summary`, `GET /v1/admin/breakdown`, `GET /v1/admin/people`, `GET /v1/admin/health` and `GET /v1/admin/activity` refuse `user_id` with `person_analytics_disabled` 400, and exports and admin recalculations refuse a person and the per-record report the same way; `POST /v1/admin/exports` serves the summary report; `GET /v1/admin/health` returns counts only. With it on, the earlier behaviour returns, limited to usage from the first full day after the switch (`person_analytics_since`). Added in both settings: `GET /v1/admin/activity` (organization summary with `days`, `sites` and `models` splits, each with `active_users` as a count) and the aggregate figures of `GET /v1/admin/people`. A member's browser list has no last-seen time, and a deletion preview or job that names one person returns no record counts or date range. See `docs/implementation/CONTRACTS.md` section 8.
- Server settings: with `ip_collection_enabled` off, `ip_key`, `ip_keys`, `active_ip_key_version`, `ip_restore_required_until` and `ip_retention_days` are not required; a settings file that still contains them keeps starting, because the keys are accepted and ignored, and `python -m aift.ip_rotation` refuses to run (`ip_collection_disabled`). `/v1/config` sends `ip_retention_days: 0` so that older extensions keep working. New one-off maintenance command `purge-network-observations` (counts; `--confirm` deletes every stored connection address row).
- `GET /v1/company/summary` and `GET /v1/admin/summary` accept `splits=none`: the same answer without the `sites`, `days` and `models` splits, which are then not computed. Other values are refused with `unsupported_filter` 422; without the parameter nothing changes. `GET /v1/me/summary` accepts it without effect. See `apps/server/API-INTEGRATION.md`.
- Environment overrides in the media policy (`estimation.media.json`, EcoLogits method definition 1.0.0) may name a `datacenter_pue` and `datacenter_wue` next to the electricity mix zone, each one decimal or a `min`/`max` range; unknown keys are still rejected, a PUE below 1 and a reversed range are refused. The TypeScript estimator and the Python server apply them alike.

- The browser database moves to version 21. A bar update that a newer update of the same reply replaced no longer records Estimate unavailable in Tracking health or reports a calculation failure to the company server.
- A browser that cannot open the extension's local database now reports Browser storage failed instead of a ledger integrity problem. On Windows this happened with a test profile whose folder path was too long; a clean install with a normal profile was not affected. See `docs/release/FRESH-PROFILE-STORAGE-HEALTH-2026-09-27.md`.
- Old chats opened from a provider sidebar now import their history without a reload, including when the chat request starts just before the address changes (Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini). See `docs/release/OLD-CHAT-IMPORT-2026-09-26.md`.
- A chat whose earlier messages are loading or not loaded no longer shows Req 0 with Unknown figures, Partly measured or scrolling advice. The bar shows the figure labels with a placeholder, and Details says what is being read or what could not be counted. Earlier activity unavailable no longer sticks after an import.
- ChatGPT and Gemini composer discovery follows their current layouts, so the bar appears again.
- History imports and conversation totals use bounded batches: at most four shared counts in flight, one HMAC key per import, and batched reads of up to 100 rows. An unchanged reimport no longer invalidates summaries.
- A reply streaming into a long chat no longer leaves the bar lagging, flipping or stuck with Tracking problem. The background replaces waiting progress updates of the same reply with the newest one, always keeps the send and finish updates, and publishes after the selected method has calculated the new figures. While a calculation is still running, the bar keeps the last complete figures, Details shows Updating in its progress line, and the bar refreshes when the result publishes. See `docs/release/LIVE-CAPTURE-AND-REOPEN-2026-09-26.md`.
- Reopening a saved chat shows its saved totals without waiting for the reimport, counts only new or changed messages, and no longer rewrites unchanged coverage or queues an empty company upload. The extension keeps each saved message's text length on this device so an unchanged message is not counted again; text is never stored and lengths are never synchronized.
- A Claude reply whose thinking appears only as a summary no longer marks the page with Tracking problem (Site response could not be observed). The live reader accepts Claude's thinking summary, thinking cut-off and tool display updates on the block they describe. Summary text is not counted as visible thinking and is not kept. Other unknown stream updates now leave the reply partly measured, as described above.

### Methodology

- EcoLogits 0.11.3 (22 October 2026, published on PyPI on 14 October 2026). The model catalog adds Claude Haiku 5.5 and Mistral Large 4 and has new measured speeds for 104 models; the calculation, electricity mixes and provider settings are unchanged. Claude Haiku 5.5 now has its own record instead of the Haiku 4.5 stand-in, an unknown Haiku version and a Claude mini, nano or lite variant use Haiku 5.5, and the smaller-model comparison for Claude and Cowork uses Haiku 5.5. The server pins the same release. For 500 output tokens without measured timing, most current models change by a few percent (GPT-5.5 -2.8%, Claude Opus 5.5 -2.5%), some older ones more (Claude Sonnet 5 -27%, Gemini 3.1 Pro -25%, o1 -51%), and GPT-5.5 Pro goes up by 48% because upstream removed its speed data. The npm package `@ecologits/ecologits.js` was not adopted: its version 3.0.0 still has the 0.11.2 data. See `docs/release/ECOLOGITS-0.11.3-2026-10-22.md`.
- The default scenario is Midpoint, halfway between the EcoLogits minimum and maximum (the same as EcoLogits' own mean of a range); Minimum and Maximum stay available in Settings. A choice already saved in an installation is kept. Details tiles show one figure; the minimum to maximum range is listed under How this was estimated.
- Output is the sum of its parts: answer, shown reasoning, tool input and other generated text, or the provider's own count where the page reports one (Claude Cowork, Claude AI artifact runs). A part that was not read leaves the total partial, never lower. Hidden reasoning is not estimated from timing. Text written by other models is estimated with its own model and added to Out.
- Generated images: 2.907 Wh of IT energy per image, the mean reported by Luccioni, Jernite and Strubell (2024), times the provider's data-centre factors. Speech: audio tokens are the length in seconds times the vendor's rate (OpenAI 20, Google 25 per second; Anthropic uses OpenAI's), estimated with the EcoLogits `gpt-4o-mini-tts` record; without an audio length, 17 characters per second. Video: the EcoLogits 0.11 video method with site defaults where the page shows no size, length or audio. NotebookLM Video Overview: one illustration per 10 seconds, each estimated as an image, plus the narration as speech. Data in `config/estimation.media.json`.
- Uploaded image tokens follow the vendors' published rules (OpenAI, Anthropic and Google), with auto detail assumed.
- Files Perplexity Computer creates: text and code files count with the tokenizer on their downloaded content as tool input of the request's orchestrator model; a file that cannot be downloaded counts as its size in bytes divided by 4 (14 October 2026).
- Family benchmarks replace the provider flagships for unnamed and unlisted models: OpenAI `gpt-6.1-sol`, Anthropic `claude-opus-5-5`, Google `gemini-3.8-flash`, and `gpt-6.1-sol` for an unknown provider. Details names the stand-in and why it was used.
- Totals add figures that were estimated alike, whatever the model. In is new input text plus tool results plus uploaded image tokens; Out is the output total plus text by other models.
- Organization totals keep one copy of each prompt or reply: a copy with exact counts first, then complete and partial live copies, then complete and partial saved copies, then copies that measured nothing. Among copies with the same evidence a live copy comes first, then the earliest. Personal figures follow the counted copy, so a reply measured live stays with its sender unless another member's copy has exact provider counts.
- EcoLogits is the default and only selectable engine for all current extension builds. Original-default build variants are retired; earlier saved preferences migrate without rewriting recorded results.
- Archived the project-developed Original method with a research history, source and audit links, limitations and reproducibility instructions under `docs/research/archive/original-method`.

- Added versioned, source-linked text and image reference registries with separate original and recalculated views.
- Unknown denominators and incompatible scopes remain unknown instead of becoming zero or a combined total.
