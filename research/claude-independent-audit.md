I found **no incorrect factual claims** in the report. The corrections below are additions and sharpenings, not reversals.

The most consequential new findings from my independent research:

1. **Anthropic's `count_tokens` endpoint is free of charge** (rate-limited: 5,000–20,000 RPM by tier) and accepts thinking blocks, PDFs, and images. The report treats counting-API calibration as an extra network cost without noting it is a zero-cost, structured-request calibration oracle. 2. **Claude 4.7+ / Fable / Mythos models use a newer tokenizer producing ~30% more tokens than earlier Claude models** (Anthropic docs, same page). Any o200k_base substitute for Claude must be calibrated **per model generation**, or consumer-site counts will be biased by roughly a third depending on which model family serves the chat. The report's "compare o200k_base and the historical tokenizer" plan is necessary but insufficient without this split.
3. **Gemini consumer models are already ahead of Google's LocalTokenizer mapping.** The [Gemini thinking docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/thinking) list gemini-3.7-flash and gemini-3.8-flash as current models; the [official loader](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/googleapis/python-genai/main/google/genai/_local_tokenizer_loader.py) maps only through gemini-3.5-flash/3.1-*. Additionally the Gemma4 tokenizer entry has **no pinned artifact hash** (it references Hugging Face `google/gemma-4-E4B-it`), unlike the pinned, hashed Gemma3 SentencePiece file. And there is **no official JavaScript LocalTokenizer** — an open feature request ([js-genai#362](https://github.com/googleapis/js-genai/issues/362)) confirms the browser port must be built, as the report assumed but did not evidence.
4. **EcoLogits' input-token omission is officially acknowledged by its maintainers** ([discussion #86](https://github.com/mlco2/ecologits/discussions/86)): they assume decoding dominates and concede they don't handle large-input/small-output requests. This converts the report's code-level inference into maintainer-confirmed fact and strengthens the case for replacing the energy kernel, not just wrapping it.
5. **Perplexity's own blog states Sonar is built on Llama 3.3 70B** ([Meet new Sonar](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/meet-new-sonar), Jan 2025, Cerebras-served), making the Llama-3 tokenizer the evidence-backed calibration candidate rather than one guess among many. Separately, the [Sonar API reference](https://docs.perplexity.ai/api-reference/sonar-post) shows a **deprecation of Chat Completions toward an "Agent API" with support ending 2026-09-27** — the calibration endpoint is churning right now — and richer usage semantics than the report records (`reasoning_tokens`, `citation_tokens`, `num_search_queries`, `search_context_size`, `cost`).
6. **The report omits Mistral's July 2025 peer-reviewed lifecycle analysis** (with ADEME and Carbone 4): 1.14 g CO2e and 45 mL water per 400-token Le Chat response, lifecycle boundary including training amortization. It is the only third-party-audited provider LCA and the best available illustration of how boundary choice moves water figures by two orders of magnitude versus Google's 0.26 mL operational-cooling-only figure. ([Simon Willison summary](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jul/22/mistral-environmental-standard/), [The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/french-ai-startup-discloses-full-lifecycle-consumption-and-emissions-for-mistral-large-2))
7. **A development-phase capture upgrade exists that the report doesn't mention:** the [`chrome.debugger` API](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/reference/api/debugger) exposes the DevTools Protocol Network domain (including response bodies) to extensions. It is unsuitable for employee production use (debugger permission, visible attach state, enterprise-policy blocks) but is the right tool for the fixture-capture prototype phase, complementing the MAIN-world fetch hook.

## 3. Detailed findings

**F1 — HIGH — Claude tokenizer generation split (new evidence).** Claim examined: report §3, "start by comparing o200k_base and Anthropic's historical tokenizer." Evidence: [Anthropic token counting docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/token-counting) — "Claude 4.7 and later models… use a newer tokenizer. The same input text produces approximately 30 percent more tokens than on earlier models." Correction: the calibration matrix must treat pre-4.7 and 4.7+/5-family Claude models as **two distinct tokenizer targets**; a single fitted correction factor for "Claude" would embed up to ~30% systematic error depending on which family claude.ai routes to. The free count_tokens endpoint counts "under the tokenizer of the `model` you pass," which makes per-family calibration cheap.

**F2 — HIGH — Gemini tokenizer coverage gap and unpinned asset.** Claim examined: §3, Gemini mapping "newer model names may be unsupported." Evidence: loader maps gemma3 (pinned artifact `gemma3_cleaned_262144_v2.spiece.model`, SHA-256 `1299c11d…`) for 2.0/2.5/3-preview and gemma4 for 3.5-flash/3.1-*; the [thinking docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/thinking) already reference gemini-3.7-flash and gemini-3.8-flash. Correction: the generic caveat becomes a concrete present-day gap — the extension will likely face consumer Gemini models with **no official local tokenizer mapping** at launch, and the gemma4 path lacks an integrity hash and points at a Hugging Face model repo (probable Gemma-license acceptance gate). Treat Gemini 3.6+ counts as "estimated tokens" (level 3), not "counted from visible content" (level 2), until Google extends the mapping. Also confirmed: **no JS SDK LocalTokenizer exists** ([js-genai#362](https://github.com/googleapis/js-genai/issues/362)); the browser port can follow the Python or [Go tokenizer](https://pkg.go.dev/google.golang.org/genai/tokenizer) reference implementations, or load the equivalent tokenizer via transformers.js-style tokenizer.json — subject to the same license diligence.

**F3 — HIGH — EcoLogits kernel should be replaced, not just wrapped (maintainer-confirmed).** Claim examined: §6, EcoLogits "cannot resolve the long-document requirement by itself." Evidence: [llm.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlco2/ecologits/main/ecologits/impacts/llm.py) — `compute_llm_impacts(model_active_parameter_count, model_total_parameter_count, output_token_count, …)`, GPU energy = output_tokens × (1.167e-6·e^(−0.0112·B)·P_active + 4.053e-5)/1000 kWh, fitted on H100-only ML.ENERGY data at fixed B=64; [discussion #86](https://github.com/mlco2/ecologits/discussions/86) concedes prefill is assumed negligible. Correction: upgrade the report's "input-aware layer" from aspiration to a specified deliverable — a two-phase surrogate E = E_prefill(context_tokens) + E_decode(output_tokens, context, batch) fitted on [ML.ENERGY v3](https://ml.energy/data/) plus [TokenPowerBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03024) (phase-aligned prefill/decode attribution, Llama/Falcon/Qwen/Mistral 1B–405B, AAAI'26, open source) plus the [Aug 2026 characterization paper's](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28044) decomposed functional form (fixed prefill cost + setup overhead + marginal per-token energy; shows per-token energy falls from 7.46 to 0.72 J/token as output grows 10→512 on H200 — a flat Wh/token coefficient is empirically wrong in both directions). Keep EcoLogits for PUE/WUE/electricity-mix/embodied bookkeeping (MPL-2.0 permits this cleanly); sanity-check the fitted curve against [Epoch AI's input-scaling analysis](https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/how-much-energy-does-chatgpt-use) (~0.3 Wh typical GPT-4o query; ~2.5 Wh at 10k input tokens; ~40 Wh near 100k — itself a model, not a measurement).

**F4 — MEDIUM-HIGH — Perplexity: stronger tokenizer evidence and an imminent API migration.** Claim examined: §3, "no verified current Sonar tokenizer artifact." Evidence: Perplexity's official [Meet new Sonar](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/meet-new-sonar) states Sonar is "built on top of Llama 3.3 70B" (Jan 2025). Still true that no current-2026 artifact is confirmed, but the Llama-3 tokenizer (128k BPE) is the *documented* starting candidate, not merely one guess. New fact: [Sonar Chat Completions is deprecated with support until 2026-09-27](https://docs.perplexity.ai/api-reference/sonar-post) in favor of an Agent API — run the Sonar calibration **immediately or against the new endpoint**, and record which. The usage schema's `citation_tokens` and `num_search_queries` fields give a provider-defined semantic model for search accounting that the extension's normalization schema should mirror (as *developer-API semantics*, still not evidence of consumer-site fields). The [third-party models legal page](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/legal/third-party-models) returned HTTP 403 to my tools (bot protection; needs the parent's browser to re-verify the current provider list).

**F5 — MEDIUM — Offset AI critique confirmed in full, with sharper details.** Independently retrieved [methodology v1.1 (April 2026)](https://www.offsetai.app/methodology-transparency-calculations). Confirmed defects, with arithmetic: (a) CO2 = 0.00034 kWh × 1.3 × 367 ≈ 0.162 g applies PUE 1.3 and a US location-based grid factor to figures whose boundaries don't allow it — Google's 0.24 Wh already contains its PUE share (0.02 Wh), so Gemini overhead is double-counted; Google's own market-based factor was 94 g/kWh, so Offset silently converts a market-based disclosure into a location-based estimate without saying so; (b) water = E × 1.3 × (1.9 + 4.54) L/kWh multiplies **on-site** WUE by PUE, contradicting the [Li et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03271v5) framework (on-site water scales with IT energy only) — and for Gemini the E already includes PUE, compounding it; (c) the "Jegham et al." citation resolves to [bovine-embryo videomicroscopy research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07945) (correct ID would be 2505.09598); (d) the claim that Jegham et al. "specifically measured this combined workload" for Perplexity retrieval is contradicted by the paper itself (30 models; OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, DeepSeek; no Perplexity, no retrieval measurement) — the 0.43 Wh Perplexity constant is unsupported.
