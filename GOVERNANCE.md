# Governance

## Maintainer

Pranit Jagannath Nale is the current project maintainer and holds repository review, merge and release authority. There are no other named maintainers or committees at this time.

## Decisions

Routine changes are decided through public issues and pull-request review. The maintainer may require additional tests, provenance, privacy review or accessibility evidence before merge.

Changes to equations, coefficients, tokenizer mappings, coverage rules or guidance require reproducible evidence, a stated applicability boundary and versioned behavior. They must not silently rewrite historical results. Privacy, schema, migration, retention, deletion and authorization changes require contract and failure-path review.

Security and conduct reports use their private channels until disclosure is safe. A private report does not grant permission to publish employee data, credentials or exploit material.

## Releases

The maintainer decides when a version is released. A release record must identify its source, hashes, schema/migration state, notices, signing/publication state and unverified external gates. The current 0.1.0 packages are unsigned candidates and are not a published production release.

## Conflicts of interest

Anyone reviewing a dependency, dataset, benchmark, paid service or methodology should disclose a material employment, financial or authorship relationship. The maintainer records the decision and may seek an additional technical review. Disclosure informs review; it does not automatically reject a contribution.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer access may be offered after sustained, accurate contributions and demonstrated care for privacy, provenance, testing and review. Any appointment is explicit and documented in this file. Contribution volume alone does not grant access.
