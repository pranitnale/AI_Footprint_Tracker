# Code of conduct

AI Footprint Tracker welcomes respectful technical disagreement and evidence-based review.

Contributors and maintainers must:

- communicate without harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks;
- respect privacy and never pressure someone to disclose employee identity, private content or credentials;
- discuss code, methods and evidence directly, without misrepresenting uncertainty or another person's work;
- preserve attribution and disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- stop sharing material when a maintainer identifies it as sensitive and use the private security channel when appropriate.

Unacceptable conduct includes doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, deliberate disclosure of secrets or employee data, and retaliation for a good-faith report.

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer, Pranit Jagannath Nale, at [pranitnale42@gmail.com](mailto:pranitnale42@gmail.com). Include only the minimum context needed to understand the incident. The maintainer may remove content, limit participation or reject contributions to protect the community and project. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, but no absolute confidentiality or response-time guarantee is promised.

This code applies to repository discussions, reviews and other project-managed spaces.
